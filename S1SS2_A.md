In 18xx Ampere characterised how a current carrying conductor produce a magnetic field in proximity as we call Ampere's law. He quantified it as: the current "I" going through enclosed closed path "C" is equal to the line integral of magnetic field "H" along the closed path "C". Then two decades later in 18xx, Michael Faraday did formulation of relating alternating magnetic field induced voltage generation in nearby closed conductor loop. Together Ampere's law and Faraday's law made transformers and Inductive power transfer made a theoritical possibility.
